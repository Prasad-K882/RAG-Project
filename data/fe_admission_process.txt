PVGCOET FE Admission Overview Academic Year 2025-26
College: PVG's College of Engineering, Technology and Management, Pune
Admission is for First Year B.Tech programs
Admission process follows CAP allotment by DTE Maharashtra
College is affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University SPPU
Institute is NAAC A grade accredited

---

PVGCOET FE Admission Pre-Reporting Checklist
Before reporting to college students must prepare the following:
Scanned copies of all original documents in jpeg jpg tiff or bmp format, maximum size 256 KB per document
Minimum 3 self-attested xerox sets of all required documents
Payment arrangement for applicable college fees
Generation of Academic Bank of Credits ABC ID or Annual Performance Appraisal Report APAR ID

---

PVGCOET FE Admission Step by Step Process
Step 1: CAP Allotment Verification at College Porch Ground Floor
Step 2: ERP Admission Registration at Room 001 Ground Floor
Step 3: E-Scrutiny and Fee Challan Generation at Room 001
Step 4: Fee Payment of College and SPPU Fees at Accounts Section or Bank Counter
Step 5: Original Document Verification at Rooms 109 and 110 First Floor
Step 6: CET or DTE Portal Confirmation at Conference Hall
Step 7: Final Reporting and WhatsApp Group Joining at Reception Counter
