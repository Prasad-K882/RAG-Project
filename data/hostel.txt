PVGCOET Hostel Overview
PVG's College of Engineering, Technology and Management provides separate hostel accommodation for boys and girls.
Hostel facilities include residential, dining, and study areas.
Hostel admission is granted for one academic year and is subject to institutional rules and availability.

PVGCOET Hostel Mess Rules

PVGCOET Hostel Refund Policy
Cancellation before final CAP round: 10 percent deduction from fees

PVGCOET Hostel General Rules
Hostel admission is valid for one academic year only
Room swapping is not allowed without Rector approval
Students must bring personal bedding items
Guests or visitors are not allowed to stay overnight
Electric heating appliances are prohibited in rooms
Hostel authorities are not responsible for loss of valuables
Celebrations, parties, and crackers are strictly prohibited
Smoking, alcohol, and tobacco use are banned in hostel
Ragging is strictly prohibited under institutional rules
